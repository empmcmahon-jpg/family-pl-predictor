How it works
Predictions normally lock at kick-off. For setup purposes, any match played today remains editable even if it has already finished. Older matches stay locked. As results arrive, correct picks score one point and both the gameweek and season tables update.
Results are refreshed from ESPN; TheSportsDB and Sporting Events supply fixture details and club badges. All kick-off times are converted from UTC to UAE local time (Asia/Dubai, UTC+4). Predictions and family names stay in this browser on this device.
